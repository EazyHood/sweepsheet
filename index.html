Take the notebook outside.

  1. Use your usual voice recorder. Put the phone away between notes. SweepSheet does not record with the screen locked.
  2. Say what you collected. “Three plastic bottles and two cans.” Use short notes and distinguish plastic from glass.
  3. Review when you’re back. Import, listen, check the transcript, then confirm. Keep uncertain counts unresolved.
